# Legal notes: input for counsel (DRAFT v0.1, 2026-10-10)

> **NOT A COMPLIANCE STATEMENT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE. REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.**
> This document is working input for a lawyer. Nothing here claims that Feelio meets any law. Where it says Feelio "does" something, read it as "is designed to support <requirement>; requires legal review" (CLAUDE.md rule 5). Do not quote any part of it publicly. No public compliance statement is drafted until counsel has reviewed it (D-013).
>
> Every legal requirement cites a source with the date it was checked. **UNVERIFIED** means it was not confirmed against the primary text: either only secondary sources (law firms, trackers) were found, or the sources disagree. Laws and dates change, so counsel should re-check before relying on anything.

## 1. Purpose and product facts counsel needs
**Purpose.** SPEC §8 asks for two things: the **strict common baseline** Feelio is built to everywhere, and a short view of **what each candidate market would add**. With both, the owner can choose launch markets knowing the cost.

**Facts (as designed on 2026-10-10):**
- **Users:** children aged 5–12 (child app) and their parents (dashboard). Only parents have accounts (Better Auth, D-002). A parent authorises a device, and the child gets a scoped, expiring, revocable device key (stored hashed). Children have no credentials, no email address and no real name.
- **Data stored** (ARCHITECTURE §7): parent identity (email, auth records); household (market, timezone, locale); a consent record; a child profile (optional **encrypted** nickname, age band, language, accessibility settings); settings; progress; usage minutes; minimal safety events (category, severity, time; any redacted excerpt is encrypted and kept 30 days); an audit log.
- **Not stored:** raw audio, full transcripts, photos, uploads. No user-to-user messaging or public sharing (CLAUDE.md rule 9).
- **Processors and locations:**
  - Vercel: hosting; functions in `fra1` Frankfurt (D-030); US company.
  - Neon Postgres: AWS Frankfurt, EU.
  - Together AI: LLM and image generation; US, no region choice (D-009).
  - ElevenLabs Agents: real-time voice; US provider; the child's voice audio is processed live (D-003).
  - An email provider for parent sign-in codes is still to be chosen.
- **Stage:** invite-only pilot. Free tiers hold synthetic or test data only. Launch markets are undecided, and market rules live in `content/markets/*.json` (`platform/markets.ts`).

## 6. Open questions for counsel
1. **Consent method.** Is `account_reauth_checkbox` acceptable for an invite-only pilot with known adult testers? Which §312.5(b)(2) method (or EU "reasonable efforts" method) should the first market use? Card transaction, knowledge-based authentication and ID checks each add a processor.
2. **"Disclose" under COPPA.** Does sending a child's voice, text or story requests to Together AI and ElevenLabs as service providers count as "disclosure" to third parties? The answer decides whether email-plus is available and whether separate consent (§312.5(a)(2)) is needed.
3. **Lawful basis (GDPR and UK).** Consent (Art. 8) or contract or legitimate interest, per purpose: profile, progress, voice, stories, safety events. Lawful basis for safety events and for the restricted household-actor events (SAFETY_POLICY §5).
4. **Transfers.** EU and UK data to Together and ElevenLabs (US): are they under the EU–US DPF or the UK extension (UNVERIFIED), or do we need SCCs or the IDTA plus a transfer assessment? Also, for MENA markets: is processing in Frankfurt plus the US workable under the Saudi, Egyptian and Tunisian transfer regimes?
5. **Parental access versus child safety.** Can we withhold a restricted safety event (household or unknown actor) from the parent's access or export request (GDPR Art. 15(4); COPPA §312.6; equivalents)? What must the consent form say?
6. **Tunisia Art. 28.** Is family-judge authorisation really needed per child for an online service, and at what age? Status of bill PLO 095/2025.
7. **Applicability thresholds.** Does the UAE CDS law (Cabinet classification), the UK Online Safety Act (chatbot amendment) or California SB 243 ("companion chatbot" definition) cover Feelio? If so, which reporting duties apply (e.g. SB 243 annual reporting)?
8. **AI Act roles.** Is Feelio a "provider" or "deployer" under Art. 50? Must generated story images be marked (Art. 50(2)), and how, given they are shown only privately to the child?
9. **ElevenLabs approval scope.** Does pre-generated static TTS played to children need the under-18 approval? What consent records must we keep for ElevenLabs?
10. **Reporting outside the US.** What CSAM or exploitation reporting duty applies in each candidate market (INHOPE has no MENA member hotline [S10])? What may we lawfully keep or preserve (US: 1 year after a report [S3])?
11. **Retention periods.** Are the proposed periods (ARCHITECTURE §7, SAFETY_POLICY §7) defensible? How long must consent records be kept ("legal hold")?
12. **Representatives and registration.** Do we need an EU or UK Art. 27 representative, an INPDP declaration, PDPC licences (Egypt) or an SDAIA registration (Saudi Arabia)? When?
13. **Pilot status.** Does an invite-only, non-commercial pilot with testers' own children change any of the above? What must the tester consent form say ("Feelio is not an emergency or child-protection service")?
14. **Public statement.** When counsel is ready: the wording of any public statement on children's privacy law (deferred, D-013).
